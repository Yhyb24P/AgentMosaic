## Note on the first attempt (not a product defect)

The first smoke attempt failed with `the lead run failed: InvalidFirstDecision`. The
script's objective had asked the Lead to delegate exactly ONE task, while the Lead's
first-round product rule requires at least two delegated subtasks. That is existing,
intentional product behaviour (a first round must genuinely delegate work rather than
trivially complete), and the objective was corrected to request two tasks. The failed
attempt's log was not retained; the decisive passing run above is the evidence.
